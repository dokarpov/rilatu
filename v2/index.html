Questions

What does Rilatu prepare?
A reviewable package: the linked agreement, invoice and payment, an index of sources and a list of what the recipient may still ask for.
How does Rilatu treat a single payment?
It links the payment to the billed period it covers and lists the remaining months as open, so the recipient sees exactly which period the record supports.
Which records does Rilatu connect?
Client agreements, invoices and bank payments. Rilatu matches them by amount, date and reference and keeps every original file as it was received.
Who reviews the package?
A qualified person on the receiving side: a landlord, an accountant or an immigration adviser. Rilatu gives them the trail behind each claim.
